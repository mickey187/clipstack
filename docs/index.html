Privacy

A clipboard manager sees everything.
This one tells no one.

Your history is a plain file on your own disk. There is no account to make and no server to sync it to.

It stays on your Mac

History lives in a local JSON file in your own Library folder. It is never uploaded, synced or backed up to us — there is no server to send it to.

No account, no telemetry

You never make an account or sign in. ClipStack has no analytics, no tracking and no crash reporting, and nothing you copy is ever transmitted anywhere.

Passwords are dropped

When a password manager marks a copy confidential, macOS passes that flag along. ClipStack honours it and refuses the item outright.

ClipStack makes two kinds of network request, and neither one carries your clipboard. It checks once a day whether a newer version exists, and it verifies your licence key when you activate and about once a month after that. The licence check goes to Lemon Squeezy, who handle payment; the update check goes to us. Everything else — capturing, searching, pasting — happens entirely offline.

The app requests one macOS permission, Accessibility, which is what lets it paste into the app you were using. That is the whole list.

Questions

Is this a subscription?

No. ClipStack is a one-time purchase of $12. There is no renewal, no yearly fee and no upgrade pricing — you buy it once and it keeps working.

What happens after the 14-day trial?

The trial is the full app with every feature, and it needs no card. When it ends, ClipStack stops capturing new items until you enter a license key. Your existing history stays where it is.

Which Macs does it work on?

Any Mac running macOS 14 Sonoma or later. ClipStack ships as a universal binary, so it runs natively on both Apple Silicon and Intel machines.

Does my clipboard data go anywhere?

No. Your history is a local file on your disk, and nothing you copy is ever transmitted. ClipStack has no account system, no sync and no telemetry. It makes two kinds of network request — a once-a-day check for a new version, and a licence check when you activate and roughly monthly after that — and neither carries your clipboard.

How do I get updates?

Updates are free for as long as you use the app, and ClipStack checks for them itself once a day. Your license covers every future version.

Can I get a refund?
