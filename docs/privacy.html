ClipStack is a clipboard manager, which means it necessarily sees a great deal of what you do. This page explains exactly what happens to that information. The short version: it stays on your Mac.

What ClipStack stores, and where

ClipStack keeps a history of what you copy — text and images — so you can paste it back later. That history is written to a file inside your own user Library folder, at ~/Library/Application Support/ClipStack/, on the disk of the Mac you are using.

It is never uploaded, synced, backed up to us, or transmitted anywhere. We operate no server that receives clipboard contents and have no ability to read your history. If you delete the app and that folder, the data is gone.

What ClipStack deliberately ignores

When a password manager or another app marks a copy as confidential, macOS attaches a flag to it. ClipStack honours that flag and refuses the item outright — it never enters the history at all.

Network requests

ClipStack makes two network requests in total. Neither carries your clipboard contents.

  • Update check. At most once a day, the app asks our server whether a newer version has been published. The request sends the version you are running so we can answer it. It downloads nothing on its own.
  • Licence verification. When you activate a licence key, the app sends that key — and the name of your Mac, so you can recognise it in your purchase account — to Lemon Squeezy, our payment provider, to confirm it is valid. It re-checks about once a month afterwards so that refunded or revoked keys stop working. If that check fails for any reason, including being offline, nothing changes and your licence keeps working.

Both can be switched off in Preferences. There is no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting and no advertising or tracking technology of any kind in the app.

Permissions the app requests

ClipStack asks for one macOS permission: Accessibility. This is what allows it to paste into the app you were previously using. It is not used for any other purpose, and declining it simply means you press ⌘V yourself.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Lemon Squeezy, which acts as the merchant of record and is the seller of the licence to you. When you buy, you provide your details to them, not to us. We receive your email address and order details so we can deliver your licence key and provide support. We never see your full card number.

Lemon Squeezy's own privacy policy governs the information you give them at checkout.

This website

This site is static. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and embeds no third-party trackers. Our host records standard server logs, including IP addresses, as part of serving the page.

Your rights

Because clipboard history never reaches us, there is nothing of yours for us to export or delete on request. For the purchase record held by our payment provider, you can contact us and we will help you exercise your rights under applicable law, including the GDPR where it applies.

Children

ClipStack is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
